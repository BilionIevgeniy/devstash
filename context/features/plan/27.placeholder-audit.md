# 27. Placeholders and skeletons that match what they stand for

## Why

- Feature 26 found the navbar's placeholder for the session grey and as wide as the guest's two buttons, while the button it stood for is light and one button wide. It had been so since `20c083e`, against the rule "a non-interactive placeholder of the same size and look" in `context/coding-standards.md` ("No flash of the wrong state").
- No test looked at it: the journeys, the axe scans and the screenshots wait for the session's answer, axe does not see an `aria-hidden` placeholder, and nothing moved, so there was no layout shift to measure. The rule was checked on review only.
- Feature 26 added `e2e/placeholders.spec.ts` for the actions of the homepage (the navbar and the hero, both answers, both themes, desktop and phone). The other transitional states of the app are still unchecked.
- Interview angle: "How do you test a state that lasts 50 ms?"

## Scope

1. Every `loading.tsx` of `app/(app)/` and every `<Suspense>` fallback (`SidebarSkeleton`, `NewItemButtonFallback`, the skeletons of the dashboard, the lists, `/settings`, `/profile`, `/upgrade`): measured against the page that replaces it, with the server's answer held back (a delayed route in the e2e build, or Playwright holding the RSC request), on a desktop and a phone and in both themes. A skeleton has the boxes of its content (height, the place of each block); several comments say "approximate" today (`settings/loading.tsx`).
2. The layout shift of each such navigation, read from `PerformanceObserver` (`layout-shift`) in the e2e test: zero, or a named exception.
3. The rest of the homepage's session-aware places: the pricing cards (`PricingSection`'s `PlanAction`) and the mobile menu's placeholder for "Sign In" (`MobileNavPanel`, a muted bar today) join `e2e/placeholders.spec.ts`, or the rule records why a bar is the right look for a text link.
4. A helper in `e2e/support/` for "hold the answer, measure, release, measure", so a new placeholder gets its line with one call; the coding standard names it.
5. What the measurements find is fixed in the same feature.

## Non-goals

New skeleton designs, a change of what a page loads, streaming more of a page.
