# 2. Legal pages and privacy

## Why

- DevStash is public, has sign-up and a pricing page with Stripe. German law expects a provider identification (Impressum) and a privacy notice (Datenschutzerklärung) on such a site. Today the footer shows "Privacy" and "Terms" as plain text without links (`src/app/(marketing)/_components/Footer.tsx`), and `docs/ai-integration-plan.md` already notes a privacy page is "needed before live mode".
- Decision for now: **portfolio mode** (ADR below). Stripe stays in test mode, the site says it is a demo, and the legal pages exist anyway: they show a German reviewer that the author knows the rules.
- Interview angle: "What does a web app need before it goes live in Germany?", "Which of your vendors are processors, and where is the data stored?"

This file describes the engineering. **It is not legal advice.** The agent builds the page structure and drafts the factual sections from the processing record, clearly marked as a draft for the operator. Ievgen writes or approves the legal text before merge (generators such as those of the IHK or e-recht24 are a common start).

## Scope

1. **ADR "Launch mode"**: portfolio mode now (Stripe test mode, demo notice, no real payments); what changes for a commercial launch (Kündigungsbutton §312k BGB, AGB, Widerrufsbelehrung, EUR prices incl. VAT, tax questions) is listed, not built.
2. **Pages** under `src/app/(marketing)/`:
   - `/impressum` (Impressum, §5 DDG; for a non-commercial site §18 (1) MStV is the fallback: the ADR notes which applies)
   - `/datenschutz` (Datenschutzerklärung, Art. 13 GDPR)
   - One static page each: German first, English below (cross-file decision 1 in `README.md`: these pages never read the locale cookie). The English part sets `lang="en"`, the German part `lang="de"` on its wrapper. The text lives as plain JSX or Markdown in `app/(marketing)/` (not in a feature: no data, no logic)
   - Both pages static, linked from the marketing footer, the sign-up page and the app shell (user menu or settings), and reachable without signing in (check the `proxy.ts` matcher)
3. **Demo notice**: a short line on the marketing page and on `/upgrade` that payments are in test mode.
3b. **Pricing honesty** (`PricingSection.tsx`, `PRO_FEATURES`): mark "Custom item types" as coming soon (deferred, `context/todo.md` "Pro features"), and change "Export (JSON / ZIP)" to "Export (JSON)" (feature 10). Same change in the plan table of `context/project-overview.md`
3c. **Footer**: Privacy (`/datenschutz`) and Imprint (`/impressum`) become real links; entries without a page (Changelog, Documentation, API, Blog, About, Terms) are hidden until they exist (`context/todo.md`, UI review)
4. **Facts the privacy notice needs**, collected in `docs/privacy/processing-record.md` (Art. 30 record of processing):
   - each vendor: Vercel, Neon, Cloudflare R2, OpenAI, Stripe, Resend, Upstash, GitHub (OAuth, avatars), later Sentry (feature 6)
   - what data, purpose, legal basis (Art. 6 (1) b contract, f legitimate interest for security/rate limits), region, retention, DPA accepted (yes/no, date)
   - cookies and device storage (§25 TDDDG): the Auth.js session cookie and the `sidebar-open` preference cookie, both strictly necessary or set on the user's own action, so **no consent banner**; any future analytics or Sentry Replay changes that
5. **Regions**: document where each vendor stores data (the Neon project is in `us-east-2`, see `scripts/require-dev-database.ts`). Moving any vendor to an EU region is a separate decision by the owner and **out of scope** here: a Neon region cannot be changed in place, and a move would change the database hosts and branch ids used in `CLAUDE.md` and the scripts
6. **AI notice**: a one-line note next to the AI buttons ("Content is sent to OpenAI when you click") and a section in the privacy notice. Check the AI Act transparency duties (Art. 50), the date they apply from and whether a postponement was adopted, and whether DevStash is a provider or a deployer; record the result in the ADR
7. **GitHub avatars**: they load directly from GitHub, so the visitor's IP goes to GitHub. Disclose it in the privacy notice (or proxy it later; out of scope here)
8. **No PII in logs**: add the rule to `context/coding-standards.md` (only ids, never emails, names or content)

## Non-goals

AGB, Widerruf, cancellation button, EUR pricing, a consent banner, analytics, i18n of the whole app (feature 11).

## Acceptance criteria

- `/impressum` and `/datenschutz` render without sign-in, are linked from every page type listed above, and pass `npm run build` as static pages
- The privacy notice names every vendor in the processing record and both cookies
- The processing record and the ADR exist; the demo notice is visible
- Lighthouse/axe shows no accessibility errors on the two pages

## Tests

None beyond the build; feature 5 adds an E2E check that the footer links work.
