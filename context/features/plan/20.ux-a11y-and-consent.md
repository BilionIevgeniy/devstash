# 20. UX, accessibility and consent

## Why

- The review of 2026-11-05, feature 13 and feature 14 left findings that a user feels: links that are not links, focus that is lost, touch targets under 44px, layout shifts, a palette that breaks two axe rules.
- All of it changes what the screen looks like, so it lands before feature 21 commits its screenshot baselines.
- A real product measures how fast it is for real visitors. In Germany that needs consent first (§ 25 TDDDG).
- Interview angle: "How do you make a list keyboard and screen-reader friendly?", "What does a consent banner have to do, technically?", "Lab data vs field data"

## Scope (commit groups)

### A. Item interaction and forms

1. **Item cards are links on type pages.** Every item on a type page has a URL (`/items/<type>/<id>`), but `ItemDrawerTrigger` is a `div role="button"` that calls `router.push`, so Cmd/Ctrl+click, "Open in new tab" and "Copy link" do not work and the route is not prefetched. Render the card as a `next/link` `<Link>` there (an `<a>` may wrap the `<article>`); keep the button on the dashboard, whose drawer has no URL. Check that the soft navigation still hits the intercepted route and that the optimistic hiding of a deleted card keeps working. Update the E2E selectors
2. **The hover colour turns off over the copy button.** The copy button of `ItemCard` and `ItemRow` sits beside the trigger (`ItemDrawerTrigger` `action` slot), so hovering it leaves the card. `group` on the wrapper when an `action` is set and `group-hover:bg-accent/50`; `FileRow`, which has no `action`, keeps its own hover
3. **Focus returns after the route drawer closes.** `ItemRouteDrawer` on the type pages lands on `body`: its `onCloseAutoFocus` calls `router.back()` and also fires on browser Back. Keep the opener in the provider of the `items/[type]` layout and focus it after `router.back()` when the list is back; after a delete or an unpin, focus the neighbouring row, not the top of the list (also in `useItemSheet`). Remove "Not asserted on purpose" in "End-to-end tests" of `coding-standards.md` and the comment in `e2e/items.spec.ts`, and assert it
4. **Inline errors in the item forms.** Only content over its limit is stopped in the browser (feature 9); a URL that is not `http(s)` (`type="url"` accepts `ftp://x`), more than 20 tags typed by hand, a tag over 50 characters still come back as a toast. An inline error under the field (`Field` with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`), a ref on `TagsField` and `DescriptionField`, the checks in `items/domain`. Do not run the Zod schema in the browser without measuring: the create form is its own chunk preloaded at idle time (`docs/performance.md`)
5. **`/profile` gets a `loading.tsx`** that mirrors the profile card

### B. Responsive and accessibility

6. **Touch targets of 44px on phones** (first: Edit and Delete in the drawer's action bar are 4px apart, and Delete is destructive). Top bar buttons 32px, sidebar links 32px, section headers 28px, collection card actions 28px, row favorite and copy 36px, drawer actions 38×32, drawer Close and Copy code 28px, footer links 18px, the nav's Get Started 36px. `max-md:min-h-11 max-md:min-w-11`, or a larger hit area through an `after:-inset-2` pseudo-element, and more space between Edit and Delete
7. **Item row titles at 375px** get about 88px. Below `sm`, the date moves to the description or tag line and the title gets `line-clamp-2`; the skeleton's `h-26` follows
8. **The top bar at 768px with the sidebar open**: the search button is 80px with only ":" of its placeholder. Icon-only search below `lg` and no "New collection" label below `xl`, or the sidebar collapsed by default below `lg`
9. **Stats cards wrap and shift**: "Favorite Collections" takes two lines at 375px and at 1024px with the sidebar open (110px against the 88px skeleton; real cards are 90px even without the wrap). A shorter label or `lg:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-4`, `min-w-0` on the label block, a 90px skeleton. Built on the one stats component of feature 16
10. **The sheet overlay barely dims the page**: `bg-black/10` in `components/ui/sheet.tsx`; `bg-black/40` or darker (a shadcn primitive shared by every sheet)
11. **The homepage code mock at 375px**: 523px of content in 341px, scrollable with no hint. Wrap long lines on phones or a fade at the right edge
12. **A skip link on the homepage**: the fixed header comes before the main content in the tab order
13. **The palette's list breaks two axe rules** (found by the `PaletteBody` stories of feature 14). `aria-required-children`: the loading skeleton and the empty message (`role="status"`), the failure (`role="alert"`) and the dimmed previous results (`div[aria-busy]`) sit inside cmdk's `listbox`, which may own options and groups only. `color-contrast`: the dimmed results take the group headings to 2.68:1 (`opacity-50`). The messages go in a live region next to the `CommandList` (keeping its height), the dimmed results become a `role="group"`, and the dim keeps 4.5:1 (or `aria-hidden` on the stale block). Then the rule overrides in `PaletteBody.stories.tsx` are removed
14. **The `Toaster` speaks the visitor's language**: Sonner's region label ("Notifications") is set in the root layout, which reads no locale. A `Toaster` in each provider layout (`MarketingFrame`, `(auth)`, `(app)`) with `containerAriaLabel` from the messages, none in the root; the static pages stay `○`
15. **The homepage calls to action know the session**: `HeroSection`, `CTASection` and `PricingCard` show a signed-in user links to `/register`. Small client links built on `useHasSession`, kept unseen until the session is known (no flash), so the page stays static

### C. Consent and real-user Web Vitals (own ADR)

16. **The ADR first checks whether the Sentry browser SDK falls under § 25 TDDDG.** If it does, the banner covers Sentry too and the SDK starts only after consent; if not, the banner exists for the measurement only. Record the answer with its sources
17. **A consent banner** mounted in each provider layout, the pattern of item 14 (never the root layout, which has no locale and keeps the static pages `○`). The consent lives only in the browser (a first-party cookie or `localStorage`, read only on the client), so no server read and no cache ever depends on it. No flash: the banner renders nothing until the stored choice is read, and never shows for one frame to a visitor who already chose. "Accept" and "Decline" equally easy; a "Privacy settings" link in the footer and the app to withdraw as easily as to give. No inline script
18. **Real-user Web Vitals behind consent**: `useReportWebVitals` in a client component, off until the visitor agrees, sending LCP, INP and CLS with the page group of `metric.navigationURL` (never the URL itself) to a thin route that logs them through `lib/logger.ts`, rate limited per IP; the grouping without Zod, so Zod stays out of every page's JavaScript (`docs/performance.md`). A portfolio has few visitors, so this proves the pipeline; before/after numbers stay lab numbers
19. **The privacy notice** (both languages) and `docs/privacy/processing-record.md` get the consent and the measurement; ADR 0014's consequences get an addendum

## Non-goals

A third-party consent platform, analytics other than the Web Vitals, A/B tests, a redesign.

## Acceptance criteria

- Cards on type pages open in a new tab with Cmd/Ctrl+click; focus returns to the opener after the route drawer
- Every refused field of the item forms shows an inline error and takes the focus
- Every touch target on phones is at least 44px; no layout shift on the dashboard at 375, 768 and 1280px
- The palette stories pass axe without overrides
- The banner never flashes, consent can be withdrawn, no metric is sent without consent; the static pages are still `○` in the build output

## Tests

- Unit: the form checks in `items/domain`, the page grouping of a URL, the consent state helper
- `dom`: the inline errors and their focus, the banner (no render before the stored choice, accept, decline, withdraw), the session-aware links
- Story tests: the palette without rule overrides
- E2E: Cmd/Ctrl+click on a card, focus return, the banner on a fresh visitor and its absence after a choice, no request to the metrics route before consent
