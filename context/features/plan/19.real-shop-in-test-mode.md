# 19. A real shop in test mode

## Why

- DevStash shows everything a real product does; the only difference is that Stripe runs on test cards. A subscription sold online to consumers in Germany has duties the current checkout does not meet: the cancellation button (§ 312k BGB), the order button (§ 312j BGB), the withdrawal rules for digital services (§ 356 BGB), a contract confirmation (§ 312f BGB), terms (AGB), prices with VAT. All of them can be built and shown in test mode; a lawyer's and a tax adviser's sign-off stay in `context/todo-before-live.md`.
- Billing operations a real shop runs: tax, a failed-payment policy, a sync for lost webhooks, and what a downgrade does.
- Interview angle: "How do you build a subscription checkout that is legally sound?", "How do you keep billing state right when webhooks get lost?", "What happens to a user's data when they downgrade?"

## Prerequisite

Feature 17 (its cron dispatcher and the HMAC rate limits) and 18 (custom types, whose downgrade is decided here).

## Scope (commit groups, each with its ADR line)

### A. The checkout session, built in one place

1. One builder of the Checkout session parameters in `billing` (services or domain, decided at the plan), so the consent, the button wording, `automatic_tax`, the tax ids and the payment methods are added there and not forked
2. **Two open Checkout tabs** (`docs/architecture.md`, Known risks): the service refuses a user who is already Pro, but not two sessions opened before either completes, and the Upgrade buttons stay live while a late webhook is pending. Restate the risk with the rebuilt session and close what the rebuild can close (for example reuse of an open session per customer)

### B. Consumer law

3. **Order button (§ 312j)**: the final button says that the order is payable ("zahlungspflichtig bestellen" in German; the English wording decided in the ADR), as far as Stripe Checkout lets the wording be set; where it does not, the step before Checkout carries it
4. **Withdrawal (§ 356 (5))**: the consent that the digital service starts at once and that the right of withdrawal ends with it, collected before payment (Checkout's consent collection or the step before), and recorded with the subscription
5. **Terms (AGB)**: a bilingual legal page like `/impressum` and `/datenschutz` (static, under the bilingual legal pages that the i18n lint exempts), the footer link, and the link at the order step. The terms state the downgrade rules of group D
6. **Contract confirmation (§ 312f)**: an email after the purchase on a durable medium carrying the terms, the withdrawal information and the § 356 (5) consent (Stripe's receipt does not carry them), through `lib/email.ts` and `billing/_internal/emails/`
7. **Cancellation button (§ 312k)**: an always available link "Verträge hier kündigen" (the label is German by law; its English context written as messages), in the footer of every page and in the app, leading to a confirmation page where the consumer cancels directly
   - **Signed in**: cancel at the period end through the billing service at once
   - **Signed out**: the form takes the identifying data; the submission is the declaration, it takes effect on submission, the confirmation goes to the address on file and notifies the account holder. Always the same answer for every input, rate limited by IP and email (HMAC identifiers of feature 17), so it reveals nothing and cannot be used to probe accounts
   - **Confirmation (§ 312k (4))** by email, with the date and time of the declaration
   - The footer link stays a plain link, so the static pages stay `○`
   - The design and its legal reading in the ADR; the lawyer's check in `todo-before-live.md`

### C. Billing operations

8. **Prices in EUR including VAT**: new Stripe Price objects in test mode with `tax_behavior`, the price ids in the env (`.env.example`, the e2e env map, Vercel), the pricing copy in both languages, `billing/domain` prices, the plan table in `context/project-overview.md` ($8/$72 today)
9. **Stripe Tax**: `automatic_tax` on the session and tax id collection; the test-mode Dashboard settings only the user can make (origin address, a registration in the sandbox) listed in the PR and the README
10. **Failed-payment policy**: decide between cancelling after the retries (today: 8 tries in 2 weeks, then cancel) and `unpaid`; if `unpaid`, `paymentIssue` and Manage Billing must also show for non-Pro users. Record the decision; the lawyer's check stays in `todo-before-live.md`
11. **Webhook reconciliation**: a daily job in the cron dispatcher of feature 17 that pages through the Stripe subscriptions within the function timeout and corrects a stale `Subscription` row, scoped by the customer (ADR 0012), idempotent, logged with ids and counts
12. **The Stripe phase 2 checks in test mode** (`context/features/stripe-phase-2-spec.md`): the real database, Stripe test mode and the browser lists that were not run, including whether the Upgrade buttons work again after Back from Stripe (bfcache)
13. Cards only: SEPA Direct Debit and PayPal stay in `todo-before-live.md` ("Live payment methods")

### D. Downgrade rules

14. **Read-only over the Free limit**: after a cancelled subscription a user with more than 50 items keeps editing them, and rewriting an old item gets around the limit. Above the limit: viewing, copying, search and delete stay; `updateUserItem` refuses text items; Edit is disabled with a link to `/upgrade`. At 50 or fewer nothing changes; favorite, pin and collections stay open. The rule in `items/domain` through `getEntitlements`
15. **Custom types of a former Pro user**: kept and read-only, their items visible; no new custom type and no new item of one until Pro again

## Non-goals

Live payments, SEPA and PayPal, invoices beyond what Stripe sends, coupons, a reseller or B2B flow.

## Acceptance criteria

- A test-mode purchase shows gross EUR prices with Stripe Tax, the § 312j button, the § 356 (5) consent, and sends the § 312f confirmation
- The cancellation link is on every page; signed in and signed out cancel, each with its confirmation email; the signed-out form answers the same for every input
- A stale `Subscription` row is corrected by the daily job
- A downgraded user over 50 items cannot edit a text item; their custom types are read-only

## Tests

- Unit: the session builder, the downgrade rules, the reconciliation decision, the schemas
- Integration, cross-user: the cancellation of A never touches B's subscription; the reconciliation writes only the row of its customer; the downgrade rule reads only the caller's counts
- E2E: the order step up to Stripe's page, the cancellation signed in and signed out, the AGB page, Back from Stripe; Stripe's hosted page is not automated
- The auth-auditor reviews the signed-out cancellation
