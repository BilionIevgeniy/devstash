# 26. A theme the user can choose

## Why

- `globals.css` holds a light theme (`:root`) and the dark one (`.dark`), but `dark` is fixed on `<html>` (`app/layout.tsx`, `app/global-error.tsx`), the toasts are `theme="dark"`, and nothing reads `prefers-color-scheme`: no user can reach the light theme
- Found in the final review pass (feature 25), whose spec asked for both themes. The pass ran in dark; light was looked at as an inventory only, with `.dark` taken off the page
- Interview angle: "How do you add a theme with no flash of the wrong one on static pages?"

## Inventory (2026-11-25, `.dark` removed, axe color-contrast at 375 and 1280)

Clean or one finding: sign-in, the legal pages, the lists, favorites, collections, settings. To fix:

- **Homepage:** the figure captions, the preview's `kbd`, the accent texts of the cards (`--type-accent-text`), all written for a dark ground
- **Cards and rows:** the date of a pinned row, the count of the active sidebar link, the avatar's initials (`.size-full`)
- **Item page and drawer:** the active star and pin (`text-yellow-400`, the type's color as text), tag badges (`bg-muted`)
- **`/upgrade`:** the feature marks of both cards
- **Buttons:** the destructive one on `destructive/10`
- Not seen by axe: Monaco's themes (a choice of their own, `VS Dark` by default), the toasts, the hex colors of the drawer's window dots

## Scope

1. The choice: system, light, dark; where it is kept (a cookie the server can read for the app, storage for the static pages) and how the first paint gets it with no flash and without making a static page dynamic (ADR 0024, ADR 0037; the Content-Security-Policy allows inline scripts, ADR 0036)
2. A control in the settings and in the user menu, and on the marketing pages
3. The inventory above, `color-scheme` per theme, the toasts, the Open Graph image as it is
4. The screenshots (`e2e/visual.spec.ts`) and the accessibility scan in both themes; Storybook's toolbar

## Non-goals

A third theme, a change of the dark theme.
